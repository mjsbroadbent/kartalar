City export for Kartalar

Downloads the streets, buildings, land use, parks and small waterways of three real cities from OpenStreetMap's Overpass API. Kartalar borrows their street plans for the Iradi cities. Each city is fetched in small tiles and saved as its own file. It takes about 10 to 20 minutes in all, mostly waiting on the server. When it finishes, save all three files and send them back.
